**Method:**
1. Read 79 station IDs from `workspace/data/michigan_stations.txt`.
2. Downloaded the NWS bulk gauge report and matched all 79 stations to their `flood stage` thresholds.
3. Downloaded USGS instantaneous gage height (parameter 00065, 15-min intervals) for each station via `dataretrieval.nwis.get_iv()` — all 79 stations returned data.
4. Aggregated to daily maximum and counted days where `daily_max >= flood_stage`, restricted to April 1–7 (I verified that USGS's inclusive `endDT` returned April 8 data, but excluding it changed no counts).
5. Kept only stations with ≥1 flood day, sorted by flood days descending.
