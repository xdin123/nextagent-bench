## What was done
- **Data**: `H1:TEST-STRAIN` from `PyCBC_T2_2.gwf` — 128 s at 4096 Hz.
- **Conditioning**: high-pass at 15 Hz → resample to 2048 Hz → crop 2 s each end (124 s) → Welch PSD (4 s segments) with `inverse_spectrum_truncation`.
- **Matched filtering**: time-domain templates (`f_lower=20 Hz`), since SEOBNRv4_opt and TaylorT4 aren't available in frequency domain in this PyCBC build. Full grid of mass1, mass2 = 10–40 (integer steps) = 2883 templates, 0 failures.

The signal is unambiguous: all three waveform models locate the merger at t≈105 s with peak SNR ≈ 17–18, and all agree on a **total mass of 44 M☉** (near-equal-mass binary, ~22+22). The strongest single template is IMRPhenomD at SNR 18.07.
